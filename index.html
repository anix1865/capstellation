Reads exactly what the Spotify or Apple Music app on this Mac is playing: song, position, pauses and skips. No account or key needed.

Start the helper in Terminal, then open the link it prints:
python3 capstellation.py

Recognizes the song from its sound, then re-checks every few seconds to catch skips. Mac audio listens to Spotify / Music directly through the helper (no microphone). Needs a free AudD API token.
